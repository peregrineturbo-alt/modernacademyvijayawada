Understanding commerce pathways in Andhra Pradesh.

This guide provides factual information about professional and undergraduate commerce courses — including curriculum structure, eligibility criteria, examining bodies, and typical career outcomes.

Sources: ICAI Scheme of Education 2023 · ICMAI Cost & Management Accountants Act, 1959 · UGC Regulations
/ PATHWAY 01

Chartered Accountancy (CA)

CA is a professional qualification regulated by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949. The course comprises three levels — Foundation, Intermediate, and Final.

Examining BodyICAI
Eligibility (Foundation)10+2 (appear after Class 12)
Minimum Duration4.5 years (incl. articleship)
Total Papers10 papers across 3 levels
Pass Criteria40% per subject, 50% aggregate
📄Source: ICAI official website · Scheme of Education 2023
/ PATHWAY 02

Cost & Management Accountancy (CMA)

CMA is a professional qualification regulated by ICMAI under the Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959. Specializes in cost accounting, financial management, taxation, and strategic management.

Examining BodyICMAI
Eligibility (Foundation)10+2 (any stream)
Minimum Duration3.5 years
Total Papers18 papers across 3 levels
Practical Training15 months mandatory
📄Source: ICMAI official website · New Scheme of Education
/ PATHWAY 03

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

B.Com is a three-year undergraduate degree offered by universities across Andhra Pradesh. Specializations include General, Finance, Taxation, and Computer Applications.

Offered ByUniversities (UGC recognized)
Eligibility10+2 (Commerce preferred)
Duration3 years (6 semesters)
SpecializationsGeneral, Finance, Taxation, Computers
Follow-up OptionsCA, CMA, MBA, M.Com
📄Source: UGC Regulations · Andhra Pradesh University Curricula
/ PATHWAY 04

Intermediate (MPC / MEC / CEC)

Intermediate (10+2) in Andhra Pradesh is conducted by the Board of Intermediate Education. MEC is the preferred stream for CA and CMA aspirants.

Examining BodyBoard of Intermediate Education, AP
Eligibility10th pass (SSC)
Duration2 years
StreamsMPC, MEC, CEC, HEC, BiPC
Best for CA/CMAMEC stream
📄Source: Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh

05 — Frequently Asked

Common questions, factual answers.

Answers sourced from official ICAI, ICMAI, and university notifications. Last verified: November 2026.

What is the eligibility for CA Foundation after 10th standard?+

Students who have passed 10th standard from a recognized board can register for CA Foundation with ICAI. However, they can appear for the examination only after completing 10+2. A 4-month study period from the registration date is mandatory.

📄 Source: ICAI · Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988
What is the difference between CA and CMA career paths?+

CA (regulated by ICAI) focuses on auditing, taxation, financial reporting. CMA (regulated by ICMAI) specializes in cost management, financial management, strategic planning.

📄 Source: ICAI & ICMAI official publications
Which intermediate stream is best for CA aspirants?+

MEC (Maths, Economics, Commerce) is generally considered most suitable. However, students from MPC and CEC streams can also pursue CA successfully.

📄 Source: ICAI · Guidance for CA Students
What subjects are covered in CA Intermediate Group 1?+

CA Intermediate Group 1 under the New Scheme (2023 onwards) includes: Advanced Accounting, Corporate and Other Laws, Taxation (Income Tax and GST), and Cost and Management Accounting.

📄 Source: ICAI · Scheme of Education 2023
Can I pursue B.Com and CA simultaneously?+

Yes, students can pursue B.Com and CA simultaneously. The overlap in subjects makes this combination efficient. Some universities offer attendance exemptions for CA students.

📄 Source: UGC Regulations · University Ordinances
What is the exam pattern for CA Foundation?+

CA Foundation has 4 papers of 100 marks each. Papers 1 and 2 are descriptive, Papers 3 and 4 are objective (MCQ-based). Minimum 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate is required.

📄 Source: ICAI · Examination Scheme 2023
What career opportunities exist after CMA Final?+

After CMA Final, professionals can work as Cost Accountant, Financial Controller, CFO, Management Accountant, or start independent practice in cost consultancy.

📄 Source: ICMAI · Career Placement Cell Data
